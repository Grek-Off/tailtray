# Code of Conduct

TailTray welcomes contributions from people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience. Participate in good faith and help keep technical work
clear, constructive, and accessible.

## Expected participation

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, reviews, commits, and other project
  spaces.
- Discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Accept good-faith correction and make room for different perspectives.
- Protect other people's privacy. Do not publish credentials, private network
  details, or personal information without permission.

Unwelcome conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
exposure of private information, and sustained personal attacks. The project
does not require anyone to continue an interaction that has become unsafe or
unproductive.

## Reporting a concern

For a private conduct report, contact the human maintainer, Vladimir Grekov,
at [grekov.vs@gmail.com](mailto:grekov.vs@gmail.com). Include only the context
needed to understand the concern and avoid sending secrets or unrelated
private data. If a public issue is appropriate, use the repository's issue
forms instead of posting sensitive details.

Reports will be read by the maintainer and handled with discretion where
practical. The project may ask for clarification, set boundaries for project
spaces, or close an interaction when needed to keep participation respectful.

This document describes project participation expectations; it is not a legal
contract and does not promise a particular enforcement process or outcome.
